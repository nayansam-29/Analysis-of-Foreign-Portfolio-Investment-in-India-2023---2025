# Analysis of Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI) in India (2023–2025)

This project presents a comprehensive data-driven analysis of Foreign Portfolio Investment trends in the Indian financial market over the period from April 3, 2023, to March 28, 2025. The study utilizes daily net investment data sourced from the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) FPI Reporting Archive and is built entirely in R, leveraging libraries such as tidyverse, dplyr, lubridate, and ggplot2 for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and visualization.
The dataset, originally comprising 7,845 rows across 8 variables, was systematically cleaned and reduced to 5,049 analytical records. Key variables include Gross Purchases, Gross Sales, Net Investment (in both Rs. Crore and USD Million), Investment Route (Stock Exchange vs. Primary Market & Others), Asset Class (Equity, Debt-FAR, Debt-VRR, Hybrid), and the daily USD to INR Conversion Rate.

Key highlights of the analysis:
1. Equity investments dominated FPI activity, with April 2023 alone recording Rs. 13,544.79 crore in net inflows
2. 2023 overall recorded a net FPI outflow of Rs. 11,238.87 crore, driven by global risk-off sentiment and US Fed rate hikes
3. Primary Market & Others accounted for 57.8% of total net investment, reflecting significant long-horizon institutional participation
4. The USD/INR rate depreciated ~6.5% over the study period (Rs. 82.22 → Rs. 87.59), with sharp outflows observed when the Rupee crossed critical threshold levels
5. Recurring March peaks in FPI inflows were identified, consistent with fiscal year-end portfolio rebalancing behavior

Tools & Technologies: R, ggplot2, dplyr, tidyr, lubridate, readr
